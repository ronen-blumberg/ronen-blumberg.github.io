Writer, Poet & Hobby Programmer

I am Ronen Blumberg — a writer, poet, hobby programmer, and independent creator from Israel.

For many years, writing was my main form of creative expression. I have written poetry, short stories, essays, and other literary works in Hebrew and English, and my work has appeared in books and literary magazines. Over time, programming became another language through which I could create and tell stories.

My Programming Journey

I began teaching myself programming seriously around 2016. After experimenting with languages such as VB.NET, JavaScript, Python, C, and C++, I found myself increasingly drawn to BASIC and to the culture of older personal computers. Today, FreeBASIC is my main programming language, alongside QB64 and other tools inspired by the DOS and home-computer era.

Writing Through Code

I am especially interested in text-based games, interactive fiction, chatbots, retro-style software, programming tools, and small experimental projects. I do not consider myself a professional software engineer. I am a hobby programmer who codes because I enjoy learning, experimenting, building things, and discovering what can be expressed through a computer.

For me, programming and writing are not separate worlds. A poem, a short story, a chatbot, or a text adventure can all begin with the same thing: an idea, a memory, a feeling, or a question that I want to explore. Some of my programs are simply made for fun; others become ways of telling personal stories or exploring subjects such as loneliness, identity, memory, technology, social alienation, and the search for human connection.

Mental Health & Lived Experience

I also live with a long-term mental health condition. I mention this not as a definition of who I am, but as one part of the life from which I create. Living with mental illness has shaped some of my experiences and some of the subjects I write and create about, while programming and creative work have given me meaningful ways to learn, express myself, and remain engaged with the world.

Personal Philosophy

I have never been very interested in following the technological mainstream. I am fascinated by BASIC, DOS-style interfaces, text-mode programs, old-school computing, independent websites, and the idea that useful or meaningful software does not have to be enormous, commercial, or fashionable.

I create because I enjoy creating.

Sometimes that means writing a poem.
Sometimes it means programming a chatbot.
Sometimes it means building a strange little BASIC game at night.

They are all part of the same journey.
